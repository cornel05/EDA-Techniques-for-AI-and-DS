5. Conclusion & The 3-Act Story

End-to-end fine-tuning achieved a 34-point improvement over the best frozen pipeline (62.5% → 96.4%). This confirms that while ImageNet features provide a solid mathematical foundation, adapting pretrained weights to the target domain is absolutely essential for cross-domain digit classification tasks.
