5. Conclusion & The 3-Act Story

The journey to solving the SVHN dataset is a classic story of data representation. We started by feeding Raw 32x32 pixels directly into classical models, yielding a fragile ~47% accuracy. The models simply couldn't untangle the foreground digits from the noisy, cluttered backgrounds.

Act II involved Feature Extraction. By pushing resized images through a frozen ResNet-18, we mapped the pixels into a 512-dimensional semantic space. This structural understanding immediately jumped our baseline up to ~63%, proving that "better features beat better algorithms."

Act III was Deep Transfer Learning. By unfreezing the convolutional layers and allowing the network to adapt its 11 million parameters directly to the house numbers, we solved the problem. The model learned to ignore the background artifacts completely, pushing our final performance to a state-of-the-art 96.32%.
